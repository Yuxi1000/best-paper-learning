To verify this claim, we finetune the model for several epochs and evaluate the model’s prediction on all responses in Dprob every 25 updates (with a training batch size of 4, the probing occurs every 100 examples). For each type of response, we average the model’s confidence on all 500 examples and report the mean value of their log-likelihood.
